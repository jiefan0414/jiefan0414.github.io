Jie Fan

I am currently a Computer Vision Consultant & Subproject Leader at ROBO Medical Technology Co., Ltd., where I develop vision and AI systems for digestive endoscopic surgical robotics. I received my Ph.D. in BioRobotics from Scuola Superiore Sant'Anna (SSSA) and the Italian Institute of Technology (IIT), advised by Prof. Barbara Mazzolai and Prof. Paolo Dario.

Before my Ph.D., I completed an M.Sc. in Control System and Automation Engineering at INSA de Lyon and a B.S. in Computer Science at Northwestern Polytechnical University, supervised by Prof. Tao Yang.

Google Scholar / Email / GitHub / CV

Research

I am interested in the intersecting fields of computer vision, robotics, geometric representation learning, and multimodal AI. I develop perception and learning methods for 3D reconstruction, uncertainty-aware sensing, and robotic autonomy, with applications in surgical robotics, biomedical imaging, bioinspired soft robotics, and intelligent inspection.

Experience
